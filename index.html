Booking Policies

A non-refundable deposit of $20 is required to secure your appointment. Please send via Zelle to 9452160084. Appointments without deposits will be canceled.

Bring your wig 2-3 days before your appointment. For braid or non-wig services, no prep is required. Hair must be braided down before arrival or a $15 fee applies.
